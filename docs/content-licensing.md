# Content licensing

## Data and art

- Data (stats, tuning, formations, text tables) is licensed CC0-1.0 where possible, so anyone can reuse it freely. Use CC-BY-SA-4.0 when attribution and share-alike should follow the data.
- Art, audio and other creative assets are licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0 unless the author chooses CC0-1.0. Do not accept assets under non-commercial (NC) or no-derivatives (ND) licences, since they cannot be redistributed with a GPL game.
- Code and content are separate: GPL-3.0-or-later covers code only, and the licence of a content file is stated next to it (a sidecar `.license` file or a field in the pack manifest).
- Only submit content you made or have the right to license. Record the source and licence of anything imported.

## Fictional by default

The data shipped in this repository uses fictional players, clubs, leagues and kits. It contains no real names, badges, logos or likenesses.

## Community packs and trademarks

Community packs are separate from the repository and may carry real names and badges. Those names and marks belong to their owners and are not licensed by Nutmeg.

- The pack author is responsible for any trademark, image and personality rights in their pack.
- Nutmeg does not host, review or endorse packs that use real marks, and does not ship them.
- Packs must state their own licence. Pack authors should not claim CC0 or CC BY-SA over marks they do not own.
- Rights holders can ask for a pack to be taken down from the places the project controls, such as links in the repository docs.

This note is project policy, not legal advice.
